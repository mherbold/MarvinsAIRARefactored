<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This page is a live “glass dashboard” for iRacing’s SDK data. Nothing here changes MAIRA behavior—MAIRA is simply displaying what iRacing is currently publishing through its shared-memory telemetry interface.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It works the same way while a <a href="https://mairapp.com/home/documentation/advanced/game-bridge/" data-type="page" data-id="1094">Game Bridge</a> is active. A bridge builds an iRacing-shaped data feed out of the other game's telemetry, and this page shows you that feed — which makes it the best place to check what a bridge is actually handing MAIRA, and where it is coming up empty.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Nothing is shown at all unless MAIRA is connected — to iRacing, or to a bridged game.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The page is split into three tabs:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Header data</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Session information</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Telemetry data</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can scroll each tab with the mouse wheel or the scrollbar on the right.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="header-data">Header data</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is a small set of top-level SDK header fields (integers) that describe the current telemetry stream and session-info block. Think of it as “metadata about the live data feed.”</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MAIRA shows these fields:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Version</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Status</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>TickRate</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>SessionInfoUpdate</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>SessionInfoLength</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>SessionInfoOffset</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>VarCount</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>VarHeaderOffset</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>BufferCount</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>BufferLength</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>TickCount</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Offset</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>FramesDropped</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">How to read this</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>TickRate: how often the simulator is publishing telemetry frames (60 Hz). MAIRA's own force feedback runs at 360 Hz on top of this — six force feedback samples per telemetry frame.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>TickCount: current frame counter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>FramesDropped: indicates MAIRA missed frames (usually because something stalled long enough that frames were skipped).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="session-information">Session information</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This displays iRacing’s Session Info data, which is a structured block of information that changes relatively infrequently compared to telemetry. In iRacing’s SDK, this is commonly represented as a YAML document containing details like weekend/session structure, cars, drivers, track info, session settings, etc.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">How it’s shown</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MAIRA renders it as an indented tree:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>property name on the left</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>value on the right (for simple values)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>nested objects/lists expand as additional indented lines</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">What it’s good for</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Confirming what session iRacing thinks you’re in</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Inspecting driver lists, car/track identifiers, session options, and other “session context” data that helps explain what telemetry means</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="telemetry-data">Telemetry data</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the big one: a live list of iRacing’s telemetry variables (the values that update every tick/frame).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>iRacing telemetry is exposed live through the SDK, and many variables are also written to telemetry logs (.ibt) at the same cadence (60 Hz).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Columns / fields shown</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Each telemetry line shows:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Offset: the byte offset of the value inside the telemetry buffer (useful for developers/debugging)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Name: the variable name (example: Speed, RPM, etc.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Index: for array variables (only shown when the variable has multiple values). Variables whose name ends in _ST are the 360 Hz ones — six samples per telemetry frame, indexed 0 to 5. SteeringWheelTorque_ST is the signal MAIRA's force feedback is built on.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Value: the current value (formatted based on type)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Unit: iRacing’s raw unit string for the variable (for example m/s, deg, or an SDK identifier like irsdk_TrkLoc). This column always shows the unit string as-is; decoded enum names appear in the Value column, and decoded bitfield flags are appended to the Description column.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Description: iRacing’s description of what the variable represents</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Friendly formatting MAIRA applies</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Booleans show as T / F and are color-coded: T is green and F is orange.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Enums (like track location/surface/session state) are displayed as readable enum names.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Bitfields are shown in hex (example: 0x00000080) in the Value column, and MAIRA appends a decoded list of the active flags to the Description text (shown in orange).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
