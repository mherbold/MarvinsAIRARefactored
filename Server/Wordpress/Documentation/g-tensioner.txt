<div class="wp-block-group"><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You will need to build the MAIRA G Tensioner to use this feature. More details on that can be found here: <a href="https://mairapp.com/home/maira-g-tensioner/" data-type="page" data-id="647">G Tensioner</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This page drives the MAIRA G Tensioner (GT) by converting iRacing acceleration data into left and right shoulder belt positions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MAIRA blends <strong>surge</strong> (forward/back), <strong>sway</strong> (left/right), <strong>heave</strong> (up/down), and <strong>seat-of-pants</strong> into independent belt commands for the left and right shoulders. Vibration effects for ABS, wheel slip, and rumble strips can also be layered on top.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In practice, acceleration usually tightens both belts, braking usually loosens both belts, cornering can tighten one shoulder more than the other, and vertical motion can tighten both belts together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The MAIRA G Tensioner is a USB serial device. MAIRA detects it and sends live position commands while you are on track.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="connection">CONNECTION</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Connect to MAIRA G Tensioner</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Connects or disconnects the G Tensioner hardware immediately. If the device is not available, the switch shows an error instead of connecting.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Connect to MAIRA G Tensioner on app startup</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When this is ON, MAIRA will automatically connect to the G Tensioner when the app starts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="calibration">CALIBRATION</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These settings define the mechanical travel range and neutral position of the G Tensioner.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Minimum angle</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The loosest allowed belt position. MAIRA will not command the motors below this angle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 60.0°</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Neutral angle</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The resting belt position that MAIRA treats as the center point between loosening and tightening.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 90.0°</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Maximum angle</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The tightest allowed belt position. MAIRA will not command the motors above this angle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 120.0°</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Max motor speed</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the maximum motor speed (in degrees per second) sent to the G Tensioner controller. Lower values make belt motion slower and smoother. Higher values make it react faster and more aggressively, and also makes the motors run hotter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 180°/s</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Inverted arms</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Reverses the travel direction of the tensioner arm motors. Turn this ON if your hardware is assembled so that the arms move the opposite way from what MAIRA expects (for example, the belts loosen when they should tighten). Leave it OFF if the arms already move in the correct direction.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Test</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Runs a calibration sweep test that drives the motors through the full range from minimum to maximum and back to neutral. Use this to verify your calibration angles are set to safe values.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="auto-tune">AUTO-TUNE</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Auto-Tune learns the G forces your car actually produces while you drive and continuously sets the Max g force for the surge, sway, and heave axes (along with the seat-of-pants scale) for you. This keeps the belts using their full range on any car without having to hand-tune each one.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>While Auto-Tune is ON, the Max g force knobs for Surge, Sway, and Heave — along with the Seat of Pants Amplitude and Curve — are driven automatically and appear disabled. Turning Auto-Tune OFF hands control back to you and restores your own manual settings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Enabled</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Turns Auto-Tune on or off. When ON, MAIRA learns and adapts the per-axis Max g force and the seat-of-pants scale as you drive.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Balance</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A triangle with Sway at the top, Surge at the bottom-left, and Heave at the bottom-right. Drag the dot toward a corner to give that axis more of the belt range while fading the others back; the center point weights all three axes equally. The seat-of-pants scale follows the sway weight, so the two rise and fall together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Centered (equal weighting)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Surge, Sway, Heave, and Seat of Pants readouts next to the triangle show the current auto-tuned values live as MAIRA learns them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Learning only happens while you are cleanly driving on the track surface above about 20 mph (32 km/h) — it pauses when you are off track, in the pits, or while crash or curb protection is active. Sudden one-off spikes in the G forces are ignored as well, so a single bump, curb strike, or light contact cannot skew the tune. Learned peaks decay slowly over time so the tune keeps adapting as grip and conditions change. The learned values are not saved — Auto-Tune starts fresh from a light baseline each time you go on track and reconverges within a second or two. Its on/off state and the balance are still remembered per car through MAIRA's per-context system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="surge">SURGE</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Surge controls how forward/backward acceleration affects both belts together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Acceleration tightens both belts</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Braking loosens both belts</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Axis mode</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Controls whether surge is active and which direction it acts. <strong>Disabled</strong> turns surge off entirely. <strong>Normal</strong> means acceleration tightens and braking loosens. <strong>Inverted</strong> reverses that relationship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Normal</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Subtract gravity</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When this is ON, MAIRA removes the gravity component caused by vehicle pitch before calculating surge output. This can reduce false belt movement on hills or steep banking.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Max g force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This sets the surge acceleration needed to reach full effect. Lower values make the belts react more strongly to small acceleration changes. Higher values require more G force before full tightening or loosening is reached. When Auto-Tune is enabled, this value is set automatically and the control is disabled.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 4.00 G</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Dead zone</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Any surge acceleration below this threshold is ignored and treated as zero. This can help eliminate small unwanted belt movements when the car is nearly still or on a straight.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 0%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Smoothing</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Applies an exponential moving average to the surge signal. Higher values smooth out rapid changes and make the belt response feel more gradual. Lower values keep the response more immediate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 0%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Curve</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adjusts the response curve shape for the surge axis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Test</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Plays a short suspension-like test signal through the belts so you can feel how your current surge settings will respond without needing to be on track.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The graph on the right shows the live surge signal. The Min g and Max g values beneath it are the smallest and largest surge acceleration seen since you last went on track, which is handy for choosing a Max g force by hand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="sway">SWAY</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sway controls the left/right weight transfer effect between the two shoulders.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>One shoulder can tighten while the other loosens</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>This helps represent lateral load transfer during cornering</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Axis mode</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Controls whether sway is active and which direction it acts. <strong>Disabled</strong> turns sway off entirely. <strong>Normal</strong> means the outside shoulder tightens during cornering. <strong>Inverted</strong> reverses that relationship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Normal</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Subtract gravity</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When this is ON, MAIRA removes the gravity component caused by vehicle roll before calculating sway output. This can reduce false shoulder bias on heavily banked surfaces.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Max g force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This sets the sway acceleration needed to reach full left/right bias. Lower values make the belts react more strongly to cornering forces. Higher values make the effect less sensitive. When Auto-Tune is enabled, this value is set automatically and the control is disabled.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 2.00 G</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Dead zone</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Any sway acceleration below this threshold is ignored and treated as zero. This can help eliminate small belt differences when driving in a straight line.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 5%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Smoothing</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Applies an exponential moving average to the sway signal. Higher values smooth out rapid lateral changes. Lower values keep the response more immediate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 0%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Curve</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adjusts the response curve shape for the sway axis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 15%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Test</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Plays a short suspension-like test signal through the belts so you can feel how your current sway settings will respond without needing to be on track.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The graph on the right shows the live sway signal. The Min g and Max g values beneath it are the smallest and largest sway acceleration seen since you last went on track, which is handy for choosing a Max g force by hand.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="heave">HEAVE</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Heave controls how vertical acceleration affects both belts together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By default, upward body loading (for example compressions or bumps) increases belt tension, while the opposite direction reduces it.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Axis mode</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Controls whether heave is active and which direction it acts. <strong>Disabled</strong> turns heave off entirely. <strong>Normal</strong> means upward body loading tightens the belts. <strong>Inverted</strong> reverses that relationship.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Normal</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Subtract gravity</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When this is ON, MAIRA removes the gravity component from vertical acceleration before calculating heave output. This is usually the most useful axis to gravity-correct.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Max g force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This sets the vertical acceleration needed to reach full heave effect. Lower values increase sensitivity. Higher values reduce sensitivity. When Auto-Tune is enabled, this value is set automatically and the control is disabled.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 1.50 G</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Dead zone</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Any heave acceleration below this threshold is ignored and treated as zero. This can help eliminate small unwanted belt changes caused by minor road imperfections.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 5%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Smoothing</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Applies an exponential moving average to the heave signal. Higher values smooth out rapid vertical changes. Lower values keep the response more immediate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 10%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Curve</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adjusts the response curve shape for the heave axis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 15%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Test</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Plays a short suspension-like test signal through the belts so you can feel how your current heave settings will respond without needing to be on track.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The graph on the right shows the live heave signal. The Min g and Max g values beneath it are the smallest and largest heave acceleration seen since you last went on track, which is handy for choosing a Max g force by hand.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="seat-of-pants-effect">SEAT OF PANTS EFFECT</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The seat-of-pants effect adds a constant force effect to the belts that is driven by the movement of the car's chassis. This gives a physical sense of the car's behavior through belt tension.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When Auto-Tune is enabled, the seat-of-pants strength is set automatically (scaled together with the sway balance) and the Amplitude and Curve controls below are disabled.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Axis mode</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Controls whether the seat-of-pants effect is active and which direction it acts. <strong>Disabled</strong> turns it off entirely. <strong>Normal</strong> applies it in the standard direction. <strong>Inverted</strong> reverses the effect.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Normal</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Minimum threshold</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The signal level below which no seat-of-pants effect is produced. Signals below this threshold are ignored entirely, which helps suppress the effect during calm driving.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 0.00</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Maximum threshold</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The signal level at which the seat-of-pants effect reaches full amplitude. Signals above this threshold are clamped to full effect.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 10.00</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Algorithm</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Selects which chassis signal is used to drive the seat-of-pants effect. Choose the one that feels most natural for your setup.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Amplitude</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The maximum belt angle deviation (in degrees) used for the seat-of-pants constant force. Higher values produce a stronger physical sensation. This control is disabled while Auto-Tune is enabled.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 120.0°</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Curve</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adjusts the response curve shape for the seat-of-pants amplitude scaling. This control is disabled while Auto-Tune is enabled.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 25%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p class="section" id="motor-output">MOTOR OUTPUT</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This section shows the live output graphs for the left and right shoulder motors after surge, sway, heave, and the seat-of-pants effect have been combined.</p>

<p>These are display-only graphs. They are useful for seeing how MAIRA is blending all axes into final belt commands. When a vibration effect (ABS / wheel lock, Wheel slip, or Rumble strip) is active, a label appears on the relevant graph to indicate which effect is running.</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="notes">NOTES</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Auto-Tune, the balance, and the surge, sway, heave, seat-of-pants, and vibration effect tuning controls all support MAIRA's per-context system, so they can be customized per car (and other context dimensions if configured).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Internally, MAIRA applies a soft limiter before mapping the combined signal into the calibrated minimum → neutral → maximum angle range. This helps prevent the final belt output from becoming too abrupt when multiple axes stack together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Vibration effects are applied as a separate oscillation layer on top of the positional output. They do not interact with the soft limiter or the per-axis dead zone and smoothing settings.</p>
