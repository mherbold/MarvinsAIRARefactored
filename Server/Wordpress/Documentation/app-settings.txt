<p class="section" id="cpu-affinity">CPU Affinity</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>CPU Affinity allows you to control which physical CPU cores this application uses. By default, Windows distributes processes across all available CPU cores, but in some cases, you may want to restrict MAIRA to specific cores for better performance or to avoid conflicts with other demanding applications - such as the iRacing simulator, streaming software, or other background processes.</p>
