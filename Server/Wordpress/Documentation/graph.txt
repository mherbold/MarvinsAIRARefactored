<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This page shows a live scrolling waveform graph of several internal signals. What you see depends on which of the 8 toggles (below the graph) you turn on.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The graph is meant for “at-a-glance” monitoring while you drive and tune.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:columns -->
<div class="wp-block-columns"><!-- wp:column {"className":"is-style-default"} -->
<div class="wp-block-column is-style-default"><!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Solo mode</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:image {"id":266,"sizeSlug":"full","linkDestination":"none","align":"right","className":"is-style-default","style":{"spacing":{"margin":{"top":"0","bottom":"0"}}}} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image alignright size-full is-style-default" style="margin-top:0;margin-bottom:0"><img src="https://mairapp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/solo.png" alt="" class="wp-image-266"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There’s a Solo button (icon) that appears when you hover over the graph.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When Solo is enabled:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MAIRA hides everything except the graph (bottom toggles and other window chrome).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The main window becomes borderless and non-resizable.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You can drag the window by clicking and dragging directly on the graph.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>This is ideal if you want to place the graph on top of iRacing as a lightweight overlay.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Click the Solo button again to restore the normal window layout.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:column --></div>
<!-- /wp:columns -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">What each toggle shows</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Each toggle enables a “layer” in the graph. If a toggle is off, that layer isn’t drawn.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Input torque</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows the 360 Hz steering torque iRacing is providing (the “input” MAIRA receives).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Output torque</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows the final force value MAIRA is sending to your wheel after processing (algorithms + effects + fades).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Input torque (60 Hz)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows the 60 Hz steering torque iRacing is providing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Input LFE</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows the current low-frequency-effect magnitude MAIRA is producing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>More information about Wheel LFE can be found here - <a href="https://mairapp.com/home/documentation/advanced/racing-wheel/#wheel-lfe" data-type="page" data-id="164">Wheel LFE</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Clutch pedal haptics</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows the current clutch haptics waveform MAIRA is generating (a live sine-based signal).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Brake pedal haptics</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Same concept as clutch, but for the brake haptics waveform.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Throttle pedal haptics</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Same concept as clutch/brake, but for the throttle haptics waveform.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Timer jitter</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows timing jitter in the playout timer loop (how stable MAIRA’s 360 Hz timing is).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">Quick reading tips</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Midline and scaling</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The vertical center is “0”.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Up/down indicates positive/negative depending on the signal.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The intensity of the line increases with magnitude.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Gutter indicators (top/bottom bars)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The graph has top and bottom “gutters” used as status indicators:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>If Output torque is clipping, the top or bottom gutter turns solid red.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>If Curb protection is active, the gutters show yellow checkers.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>If Crash protection is active, the gutters show orange checkers.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This makes it easy to spot clipping/protection events without staring at the waveform.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
