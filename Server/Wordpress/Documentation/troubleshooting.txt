<p class="section">Logitech Wheelbases</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Logitech wheelbase users <strong>must</strong> also disable the iRacing simulator’s support for TrueForce. To do this, edit the iRacing <strong>app.ini</strong> file which is located in your documents iRacing folder, and find and replace <strong>loadTrueForceAPI=1</strong> with <strong>loadTrueForceAPI=0</strong> and save.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">Loss of FFB</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you are experiencing issues with the FFB going away, iRacing might be trying to steal back the FFB. To stop that from happening, you can edit the iRacing <strong>app.ini</strong> file which is located in your documents iRacing folder, and find and replace <strong>resetWhenFFBLost=1 </strong>with <strong>resetWhenFFBLost=0</strong> and save.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">App Crashes</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If MAIRA crashes or behaves unexpectedly, the most helpful thing you can do is grab the log file. Every time it runs, MAIRA writes a new log file to <strong>Documents\MarvinsAIRA Refactored\Logs</strong>, named after the date and time it started (for example <strong>MAIRA - 2026-07-04 14-30-15.log</strong>). Grab the most recent one — it records what the app was doing leading up to a problem, so it is usually the fastest way to figure out what went wrong. MAIRA keeps the last 7 days of logs and removes older ones automatically.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you report a crash, please attach this log file. If you can, reproduce the crash first and then grab the log right afterward, so it captures the failure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can report crashes (and anything else) on the <a href="https://discord.gg/Y7JN3BAz72" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Discord Server</a> or on the <a href="https://forums.iracing.com/discussion/72467/marvins-awesome-iracing-app" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">iRacing Forum</a>. Including the log file with your report makes it much easier to diagnose and fix the issue.</p>
