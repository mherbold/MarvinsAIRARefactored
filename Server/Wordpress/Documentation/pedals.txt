<p>This page drives pedal haptics by generating vibration commands for Simagic HPR devices connected to the clutch, brake, and throttle pedals.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This works for any Simagic pedal haptic device (P-HPR, P-HPR GT, P-HPR NEO) connected to P500, P700, or P1000 pedal bases, or the P2000 Haptic Control Box.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MAIRA can run up to 3 “effect slots” per pedal. For each pedal, MAIRA checks slot 1 → slot 2 → slot 3 and plays the first effect that is currently active (first-match wins).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are also global shaping controls (frequency/amplitude ranges + curves) that affect most effects.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The same pedal effects you configure on this page can also drive non-Simagic pedal haptics through SimHub.  MAIRA ships with a SimHub telemetry plugin that publishes each pedal's live effect frequency and amplitude, so SimHub can play those effects on any pedal hardware it controls.  This is set up inside SimHub (using MAIRA's telemetry plugin), not on this page.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p class="setting">Maximum</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The ending (top-most) frequency for effects.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 50 Hz</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Curve</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This defines how MAIRA should transition (ramp) from minimum to maximum frequency.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>0% (OFF) means to use a linear (straight) transition from minimum to maximum</li>

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Larger positive numbers mean to transition more slowly from minimum to maximum</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Larger negative numbers mean to transition more quickly from minimum to maximum</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="rpm">RPM</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Vibration that increases as RPM approaches shift RPM.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Starting RPM</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the percentage of the shift RPM to start vibrating at.  For example, if the shift RPM of the current car is 6000 RPM and you set this to 50%, then the pedal will start vibrating at 3000 RPM.  The default value of 100% means to vibrate only when the engine is at shift RPM.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 100%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Vibrate In Top Gear</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Since you can't shift up when you are already in top gear, it makes little sense to vibrate the pedal if you are in top gear, so the default is OFF.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Fade With Throttle</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When this is ON, the effect amplitude is modulated by the throttle pedal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="shift-rpm">Shift RPM</p>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A shift-light-style effect that vibrates the pedal once you reach the shift point, cueing you to change up.  It plays when engine RPM is at or above the car's shift-light RPM and you are not already in top gear.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Frequency</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 100%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Amplitude</p>

<p class="default">Default: 100%</p>
