<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is a list of all the features that are currently in MAIRA.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">General Features</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Most settings can be saved per car, per track, per track configuration, and per wet/dry condition — MAIRA works out which values apply and loads them for you as you get in the car</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Ability to control most settings to various inputs (keys, knobs, buttons, hats)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Controller profiles - save and switch between named sets of button/input mappings</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Tuning profiles - see every per-car, per-track and wet/dry set of settings MAIRA has saved, what each one changes, and revert, copy, promote or clean them up</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Automatic timestamped settings backups, with recovery at startup if the settings file is ever damaged</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>SimHub plugin</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">Awesome force feedback experience - the main feature!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>360 Hz force feedback for all wheelbases — iRacing's own native rate, on a high-resolution timer</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Works for all types of wheelbases - gear driven, belt driven, and direct drive</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The FFB graph — everything MAIRA does to the force feedback signal is built out of modules wired together in a graph, so you can see the whole signal path and change any part of it</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Four built-in graphs to start from, plus as many of your own as you like — copy one, edit it in the node editor, and export or import graphs to share them</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>You never have to open the node editor: each graph brings its most useful controls out into a quick-controls section, and you choose which ones those are by pinning them</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Strengths are in Nm at the wheel rather than percentages, so a number means the same thing on any wheelbase</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Modules for every part of the signal chain -<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Sources: 60 Hz and 360 Hz steering torque, wheel LFE, wheel velocity, friction, soft lock, and wheel centering</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Signal processing: gain, low pass and high pass filters, compressor, slew limiter, slew compressor, transient enhancer, adaptive smoother, interpolator, and prediction</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Mixers: add, subtract, blend, adaptive blend, and A/B switch</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Effects: crash protection, curb protection, speed gain, torque dither, and the understeer / oversteer / seat-of-pants wheel forces</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Vibrations: ABS, gear change, shift RPM, engine RPM, road texture, slip texture, and understeer / oversteer / seat-of-pants</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Output: curve shaping and a soft limiter, so the approach to full force is squeezed instead of clipped</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Prediction removes telemetry latency, adjustable in milliseconds</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Any module's knobs, switches and on/off toggle can be mapped to a button, so you can adjust or disable a single effect from the cockpit</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Record up to five minutes of a drive at the full 360 Hz, then replay it through the graph in the preview and tune your force feedback with the sim closed — with a track map, a hover data card, and zoom and scroll over the trace</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Coming from an older version? Your algorithm choice becomes the matching built-in graph and your tuned values are carried across for you</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>RPM lights on Logitech TrueForce wheels (G PRO, RS50, G923)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">Game bridge</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Use MAIRA with racing games other than iRacing (experimental)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Supports Le Mans Ultimate, Assetto Corsa, Assetto Corsa Competizione, Assetto Corsa EVO, Assetto Corsa Rally, rFactor 2, and RaceRoom Racing Experience</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Reads each game's shared memory directly - only rFactor 2 needs a (free, community) plugin</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Telemetry is sampled on MAIRA's own 360 Hz clock and fed through the same FFB graph iRacing uses, so every module works the same way in any supported game</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Connects and disconnects on its own as the game starts and stops</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
