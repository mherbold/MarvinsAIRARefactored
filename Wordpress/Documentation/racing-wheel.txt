<p class="section" id="overall-strength">Overall Strength</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These settings define your overall torque scaling.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Wheel Force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Your wheel’s physical maximum torque (Nm).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Set this to your wheel’s rated peak torque</strong> (example: 10 Nm, 15 Nm, 25 Nm, etc). MAIRA uses this to keep units consistent and to compute other values correctly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Here is a handy table of <a href="https://mairapp.com/wheel-force-settings/" data-type="page" data-id="451">Wheel Force Settings</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Strength</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Overall strength multiplier (0–100% normally).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the “how strong should it feel” knob. This remaps the iRacing car steering wheel torque into a smaller value that will fit on your wheelbase without clipping.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For example, the Mazda MX-5 Cup car tends to produce approximately 20 Nm peak force on the steering wheel. If you only have a 10 Nm wheelbase, this is too much! So, for this situation you would set <strong>Strength</strong> to 50% to make the 20 Nm force fit on your 10 Nm wheelbase without clipping (50% of 20 Nm = 10 Nm).</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Max Force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is the “target peak torque” MAIRA uses internally for normalization. It’s tightly linked to the two settings above.</p>

<p><strong>Important:</strong> MAIRA keeps these three values in sync:</p>

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Changing Wheel Force or Strength will update Max Force automatically.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Changing Max Force will update Strength automatically.</li>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="auto-strength-feature">Auto-Strength Feature</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The auto-strength feature allows you to calibrate the strength of the force feedback to the car/track you are driving, in an automatic way. This is how you would use this feature -</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Set the <strong>Auto Target</strong> number</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Get on the track</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Hit the clear button</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Drive around the track normally for at least a few corners<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Do not hit curbs or go off track</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Hit the set button</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>All done, you're ready to go!</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Note - The auto-strength feature adjusts the strength of the force feedback only when you hit the <strong>Set</strong> button!</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Auto Target</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Set this to be how strong you want the main weight of the force feedback experience to be, when using the auto-strength feature.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you have a 20 Nm wheelbase, setting this to 50% means you only want to use half (10 Nm) when taking corners.  The rest is reserved for spikes (curb strikes, bananas, collisions, etc.)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You may want to set this to higher than 100% if you have a weak wheelbase, however this does mean the force feedback signal will clip when taking intense corners.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Set</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When you press the <strong>Set</strong> button, the auto-strength feature goes ahead and sets the <em>Max Force</em> to the measured max force (Auto Max Force) value.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Clear</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Clears the measured max force value, so MAIRA can “re-learn” from fresh driving.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Current Force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This shows the strongest raw torque MAIRA has observed, based on live steering torque from iRacing, while on-track.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Auto Max Force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The measured max force, that will be applied to the Max Force setting if you hit the <strong>Set</strong> button.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="algorithm">Algorithm</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This section controls <em>how MAIRA processes iRacing’s steering torque signal</em> before sending it to your wheel.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Algorithm</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Available algorithms:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Native 60Hz</strong>: Uses iRacing’s 60 Hz torque signal (simplest).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Native 360Hz</strong>: Uses iRacing’s higher-rate steering torque samples (more detail).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Detail Booster</strong>: Boost details for more road feeling</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Delta Limiter</strong>: Cuts down on details for a smoother feeling</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Detail Booster (Low Latency)</strong>: Same as detail booster, but with lower latency</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Delta Limiter (Low Latency)</strong>: Same as delta limiter, but with lower latency</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Slew and Total Compression</strong>: Reduces aggressive increases in torque (slew) and decreases large torque output (total)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Multi Adjustment Toolkit</strong>: Selects FFB type, adjusts the amount of high frequency FFB (360 Hz Detail - 360 Hz on 60 Hz FFB only), decreases large torque output (Torque Compression), reduces aggressive increases in torque (Slew Rate Reduction), decreases or increases FFB dynamics (Detail Gain +/- Adjustment), and smooths FFB (Output Smoothing)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Detail Booster (Low Latency)</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Soft Clipping</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Applies a soft limiter near the extremes so the signal eases into ±100% instead of “brick wall” clipping.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Prediction Mode</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Predicts the next steering-torque sample to reduce latency and improve responsiveness.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Disabled</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Predict K1</strong> (predicts 1 frame ahead)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Predict K2</strong> (predicts 2 frames ahead)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: Predict K1</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Prediction Blend</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How much predicted torque is blended in:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>0% = no prediction effect</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>100% = full prediction</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 35%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Detail Boost</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Boosts rapid torque changes to increase “texture” and small details.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 50%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Detail Boost Bias</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How quickly the boosted signal is pulled back toward the base signal. Higher bias = less “ringing” / less exaggerated detail.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 10%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Delta Limit</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Limits how fast torque is allowed to change per step. Helps reduce harsh spikes and oscillation, and makes the driving feel smoother.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 500 Nm/s</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Delta Limiter Bias</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Bias toward the raw signal. Higher bias = follows the raw torque more closely.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 20%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Slew Threshold</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Threshold at which slew compression starts clamping fast torque changes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 0.02% of Max Force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Slew Compression Rate</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How strongly fast changes are compressed once above the threshold.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 65%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Total Threshold</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Threshold where total compression starts acting on the overall signal.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 65% of Max Force</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Total Compression Rate</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How much the overall signal is compressed beyond the Total Threshold.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 75%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Torque Compression</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Compresses high-torque peaks to reduce clipping and keep more usable midrange.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Slew Rate Reduction</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Reduces how quickly torque can drop (and in some cases rise), smoothing sharp transitions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Detail Gain</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Boosts deviations from the “steady” component of the signal to enhance road detail.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Output Smoothing</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Adds smoothing to the final output to reduce chatter / jitter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
