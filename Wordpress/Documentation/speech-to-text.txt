<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This feature lets MAIRA show live speech-to-text (STT) captions from iRacing voice chat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MAIRA now uses the ElevenLabs Speech-to-Text service for transcription. It listens to your selected recording input, sends short audio segments while radio traffic is active, and shows captions in the STT overlay window.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You still need your iRacing audio routing set up correctly (for example with VB-CABLE) so voice chat audio reaches the recording input you selected in MAIRA.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://mairapp.com/home/documentation/other-stuff/vb-cable-setup/" data-type="page" data-id="282">VB-CABLE Setup</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="speech-to-text">SPEECH TO TEXT</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These settings control live captions for iRacing voice chat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Enabled</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Turns speech-to-text on/off.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When enabled, MAIRA starts audio capture, sends voice-chat audio to ElevenLabs for transcription, and updates the overlay with partial/final text as radio messages come in.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Recording Device</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Chooses the Windows recording input MAIRA listens to for STT. In most setups, this should be the input that carries iRacing voice chat audio.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If your captions are blank or unreliable, this is the first setting to check.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="account">ACCOUNT</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These settings manage your ElevenLabs API access for speech-to-text.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">API Key</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Enter your ElevenLabs API key. MAIRA uses this key to send STT audio and check account usage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Verify Key</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Checks whether your key is recognized and whether it has the required STT/account permissions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Usage</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows your current ElevenLabs subscription usage so you can keep an eye on limits.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section" id="overlay-window">OVERLAY WINDOW</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These settings control how the live STT caption window looks and behaves.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Show the overlay window title</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Shows or hides the title bar on the STT overlay.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: ON</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Make the overlay window draggable</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When ON, you can drag the overlay to a new position. When OFF, the overlay becomes click-through so it doesn’t block your mouse during racing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: OFF</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Overlay Window Scale</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Changes the size of the STT overlay text window.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"default"} -->
<p class="default">Default: 100%</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
