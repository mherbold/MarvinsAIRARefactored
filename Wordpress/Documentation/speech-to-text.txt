<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This feature lets MAIRA display live speech-to-text (STT) captions from iRacing voice chat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>MAIRA does not do speech recognition itself. Instead, it launches a small Chrome/Edge “Speech-To-Text Bridge” browser window that uses the browser’s built-in speech recognition (Web Speech API) and streams the recognized text back to MAIRA over a local WebSocket connection.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Note: You must setup VB-CABLE for this feature to work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://mairapp.com/home/documentation/other-stuff/vb-cable-setup/" data-type="page" data-id="282">VB-CABLE Setup</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"section"} -->
<p class="section">Speech to Text</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"setting"} -->
<p class="setting">Enabled</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Turns the speech-to-text system on/off.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>What happens when you enable it</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MAIRA checks that you are connected to iRacing.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MAIRA verifies Chrome or Edge is installed.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MAIRA starts a small local web server on your PC and launches the STT browser window to http://localhost:18888/</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>MAIRA waits for the browser page to connect back (up to ~10 seconds).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Once connected, MAIRA sets the initial language and you’re ready to start recognition.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Important</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Leave the browser window open. (Closing it breaks the STT connection.)</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Click Enable Speech-To-Text.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>When the browser asks, click Allow microphone access.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Confirm the browser is using the correct microphone input device (the VB-Audio Virtual Cable).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
